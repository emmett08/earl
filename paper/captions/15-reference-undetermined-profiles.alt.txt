A directly labelled position plot of six disjoint profiles of authored synthetic reference states. Stale and invalidated each account for 128 recipient references from two unique task/time positions. Missing observation, invalidated plus stale, scope/version mismatch, and scope/version mismatch plus stale each account for 256 references from four unique positions. The twenty positions expand through 32 trajectories per arm and two arms to 1,280 references, 640 per arm. Mixed profiles include historical fallback reasons and do not estimate independent causes or deployment prevalence.
