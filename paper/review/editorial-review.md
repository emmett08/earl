# Editorial review

Applied `write-engineering-analysis`, `humanise-agentic-writing` and `audit-engineering-wording` to manuscript prose, captions and table descriptions. Argument reconstruction informed the claim/evidence record; its internal labels are kept outside the manuscript.

Material repairs during review:

| Location | Wording or inference reviewed | Revision and preservation check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abstract | A computation “gives observations their force” | Replaced the metaphor with “the rule used to interpret those observations”; preserved the observation-to-decision relation. |
| Introduction | “Relevant evidence” | Named evidence for the specified deployment and interval. |
| Method | All conditions “receive raw reports” | Corrected the interface description: models receive a catalogue and collector-derived metrics/facts; the independent reference reads raw rows. EAL adds checked assessment. This correction narrows what the experiment tests. |
| Method and worked example | Possible transfer from pilot results to all EAL features | Explicitly identified `structured/1`, supplied source and untyped example; the pilot does not test typed binding, objections or model authoring. |
| Semantics | “8,192 nodes and claims” | Replaced with 4,096 argument nodes and 4,096 claims across 8,192 rounds. |
| Results | Host correctness | Attributed 141 agreements to archived comparison flags; the reanalysis does not rerun the historical host. |
| Results | Full-model 40 versus 36 | Kept composite correctness separate from identical 40/40 status accuracy. |
| Statistical interpretation | “Estimates realised yield” | Changed to “describes realised yield”, keeping the finite-case denominator and missing-outcome boundary. |
| Captions | Constructed values versus measured results | Explicitly identified the two explanatory figures, stipulated perturbation and historical empirical matrices. |
| Throughout | Repeated contrast forms | Replaced optional exclusions with positive interface or sampling descriptions. Retained necessary non-implications, missingness limits and semantic negation. |

The final surface scans nominated 159 style candidates and 125 wording candidates. Those counts include LaTeX `label` commands, literal graph labels, model names, method identifiers and repeated technical nouns; they are not counts of confirmed errors. Context review retained the following senses:

- `label`: LaTeX cross-reference identifier or explicitly defined graph acceptance value.
- `record`: named source record, collected observation, request row or archived trial, determined by its local subject and producer.
- `typed`: proposition/input-schema correspondence, defined in Section 3.3; the fixed-source pilot is explicitly distinguished.
- `control`: controlled loopback workload/fault injection, runtime resource limit, or experimental comparator/access condition.
- `profile`: the bounded EAL semantics or the dated MCP interaction profile, with source/version identified.
- `supported`, `correct`, `available`, `unresolved`: named graph, engineering, scoring or availability predicates, with local definitions and denominators.
- `GPT-4.1`, `GPT-5.4`, `SHA-256`: established model and digest identifiers; model snapshot dates and settings are supplied in the method.

The three introductory research questions organise the study and are answered across the paper; they are not an ornamental immediate question-and-answer exchange. The eight method names are deliberately repeated as exact versioned identifiers. Negatives about factual truth, physical interpretation, missing answers and population inference are retained because removing them would strengthen the claims.

The reader-order pass checked definitions of subject/quantity/scope/interval/query, argument and claim labels, availability, composite correctness, paired difference and completed-pair exposure. Captions repeat the distinctions needed when figures are read independently. A final source check and rendered-page inspection found no further material wording change. This record does not represent an independent multi-reviewer publication assurance process.
