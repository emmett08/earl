# EAL/2 cognitive-bias attribution and agent-induced error study

**Protocol version:** 0.1.0 (24 September 2026). **Readiness:** developmental calibration; the independent confirmation and human trial below are specified proposals, not completed experiments. The execution record must name the exact source commit, case manifest digest, prompts, model response identities, reviewer decisions and any amendments. Freeze these before the first call in each cohort. This study does not change EAL/2 syntax or the meaning of an EAL claim status.

## Question and limits of the claim

EAL/2 makes an engineering claim, its evidence, warrant, assumptions and objections inspectable and executable within its specified methods. Its checked `supported`, `contested`, `unsupported` or `out_of_scope` status concerns the supplied argument and evidence. It does **not** reveal whether a person used a cognitive shortcut. The founding human-facing proposition is that a structured argument may help an engineer challenge a fluent AI answer before relying on it. The present API experiment asks a narrower question: **Can an OpenAI agent distinguish a supported, specified bias-mechanism hypothesis from a technical defect, a justified decision and an insufficient record, while correcting the underlying defect without creating a fresh error?**

The population for the developmental result is the particular synthetic, author-created EAL/2 case families in `cases.json`. It is neither a sample of engineering decisions nor evidence that real people have been debiased. An independent, prospective human trial is needed for the human-facing proposition. The 96-, 960- and 800-call EAL/2 studies assessed other host, reasoning and delivery questions; their calls, variants and author-created roots are not additional independent bias cases.

The competing agent hypotheses are: **H1**, a challenger that independently inspects rivals improves fully warranted disposition over a direct critic without an unacceptable rise in false attribution or introduced engineering errors; **H2**, apparent improvement is generic extra model compute or conservative abstention, rather than rival-specific scrutiny; and **H3**, cue sensitivity, evidence insensitivity or authority agreement creates new systematic model errors. A call-matched independent repetition in Stage B distinguishes H1 from extra compute; reporting supported-case recall and resolution coverage alongside false attribution distinguishes useful caution from indiscriminate abstention. Stage A describes pragmatic one-call versus two-call routes and **does not identify the contribution of topology apart from extra computation**. Stage A's cases and analyses are exposed developmental material, so confirmatory thresholds and the winner must be fixed before Stage C.

## Construct and reference decision

A **bias hypothesis** names a mechanism, a cue, an affected operation (search, weighting, confidence, choice or reconstruction), a predicted directional signature, a defeating observation and credible rivals. Separately record a wrong configuration, missing or stale observation, ambiguous requirement, constrained authority, workload, incentive or rational loss asymmetry. A poor outcome or a disagreement is insufficient to attribute a human bias.

For a bounded decision episode, code the evidence chain in this order: exposure to the cue **E**; genuine opportunity to do otherwise **O**; uptake into the person's actual grounds **U**; the mechanism-specific directional signature **S**; temporal order **T**; a matched, repeated or randomised contrast **C**; and examination of credible rivals **R**. A case-level attribution requires the available conjunction, with the strength of `C` and causal identification determining the status. The permitted status vocabulary is `NOT-APPLICABLE`, `HYPOTHESISED`, `COMPATIBLE`, `EPISODE-SUPPORTED`, `COMPARATIVELY-SUPPORTED`, `CAUSALLY-SUPPORTED`, `REJECTED` and `UNDECIDED`. A synthetic script may specify a record to which this coding rule applies; it cannot establish that a real person's private cognitive process occurred. In particular, reserve `CAUSALLY-SUPPORTED` in real episodes for a defensible intervention and do not infer an individual's bias solely from a group effect.

The reference record for each case contains (i) the EAL/2 source and identified synthetic records; (ii) the independent brief and any evaluator result for the engineering claim, if acquisition and assessment actually ran; (iii) a time-ordered reviewer/actor trace, if one exists; (iv) the cue, opportunity, uptake, signature, contrast and rival evidence with stable span IDs; (v) the strongest permitted bias status and admissible alternative statuses if evidence is genuinely equivocal; and (vi) a safe engineering correction. Neither the model prompt nor the host's EAL result may contain the private gold bias label. A host status can be supplied as a **controlled experimental factor**, provided every compared arm receives equivalent engineering information. Never score agreement with a host status as proof of psychological inference.

**Content-validity gate:** static parsing/type checking alone establishes only that source text is well formed. The current generator declares a `bias_hypothesis` claim, `hypothesis_route`, process-record evidence keyed by external ledger IDs, and an `alternative_challenge` for rival, missing-link and insufficient variants. Its separate `decision_claim` has a `defect_challenge`. An interim check using the full EAL/2 parser and typechecker accepted all 48 generated sources with zero diagnostics; rerun and retain that check against the **final frozen generator and case digest**. Review the source-to-ledger correspondence and gold exclusion separately. The source is an **authored static candidate graph** referring to a synthetic process ledger; there are no acquisition envelopes and no host psychological evaluation. `structured/1` checks an authored relation under supplied records, and neither its status nor static validity proves that a human bias occurred. Stage A can test the agent's treatment of that static EAL/2 argument plus external ledger; it cannot isolate the effect of EAL notation from the prose ledger without Stage B's matched representation control.

## Cases and discriminating controls

The developmental manifest fixes twelve **families**, not forty-eight independent observations. Each family offers four variants: `process` (a mechanism-specific trace with exposure, opportunity, uptake, temporal and directional evidence, and an eligible contrast); `rival` (the same apparent defect/outcome explained by a technical or organisational cause); `missing_link` (one of three explicitly recorded missing premises); and `insufficient` (a plausible signature without enough process or rival evidence). Rotate `missing_link` across four families with cue absent (`E=0`, `NOT-APPLICABLE`), four with exposure but no actual choice opportunity (`E=1,O=0`, `NOT-APPLICABLE`), and four with exposure and opportunity but a contemporaneous independent decision basis excluding cue uptake (`E=1,O=1,U=0`, normally `REJECTED`). These are distinct diagnoses even when the resulting recommendation is the same. The fixtures must preserve the engineering question, available source records and outcome across matched variants except for their declared manipulation. Any variant that changes a relevant observation or the actor's authority is a separate **relevant-information control**, not an allegedly neutral paraphrase. A valid argument with a plausible but defeated objection tests wrongful overturning.

Use different bounded engineering episodes across families: resource-capacity estimates with irrelevant initial numbers (anchoring); confirming and disconfirming tests **matched on diagnostic strength and access** (confirmation); false AI release recommendations and absent alerts with specified alert coverage (automation commission and omission); a senior-reviewer label that conveys **no relevant expertise or new evidence** (authority); extensionally equivalent failure/success descriptions with fixed payoffs (framing); applicable incident base rates and likelihoods (base-rate use); forecast intervals with known historical coverage (overprecision); sunk migration effort with **identical prospective costs and consequences**; ex-ante review with subsequent good/bad outcome held separate (outcome bias); reconstructed forecasts with preserved pre-outcome records (hindsight); and group review with shared and uniquely held material evidence. A named mechanism is only a hypothesis until the recorded contrast supports its predicted direction. If expertise, evidence quality, future prospects, payoffs or source accessibility differ, the changed answer may be rational and the proposed bias contrast fails. Scripted traces, where used, must say they are synthetic. Keep these author-created families in development; commission fresh, independently authored families for confirmation.

Mechanism-specific negative and positive controls must alter interpretation. **Stage A implements the scripted process, technical rival and missing-link cases.** Its variants also include uncertainty records; it does not include a within-family cue-only swap, a style experiment, a changed material observation, a prompt-boundary attack or a verified valid-argument overturning pair. The other controls in this table are requirements for the new Stage B/C cohorts, not outcomes of the current 48 cases:

| Control | Fixed fact and manipulation | Failure diagnosed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irrelevant-cue pair | Keep evidence and normative decision fixed; swap an initial number, non-informative authority label, author preference or evidence order | Model decision follows the cue rather than relevant grounds |
| Fluency perturbation | Keep propositions and evidence fixed while varying style; independently measure comprehension, verification time and perceived confidence | A decision shift could follow verification cost rather than simple attraction to polished prose |
| Relevant-evidence pair | Change a material observation, time, scope or objection and recompute the checked status | Apparent cue invariance is mere insensitivity to evidence |
| Technical-rival pair | Match adverse outcome and surface language; change sensor coverage, stale record, configuration or authority constraint | Bad outcome is mislabeled a cognitive mechanism |
| Missing-link cases | Separate no exposure, no opportunity and evidenced no uptake, with distinct expected statuses | Presence of a cue or a poor outcome is mistaken for reliance |
| Valid-argument case | Add a tempting but answered objection without changing support | An agent introduces a wrong correction through overzealous criticism |
| Prompt-boundary case | Put a purported instruction in an untrusted evidence field | Source text is promoted to application authority |

For human-bias **existence**, real contemporaneous traces and eligible opportunities are indispensable. For a **causal cue effect**, assign the irrelevant cue randomly across independent human reviewers and counterbalance substantive cases; a shift in group decisions is evidence for the manipulated cue under the assignment assumptions, while individual diagnosis still requires process evidence. An agent's response to a cue estimates a property of that model system, not human bias.

## Staged agent design

**Stage A: executable calibration.** Freeze `cases.json`, the answer contract and the scoring rules. The scheduled design has 12 families × four cases × three requested models × (one direct call + two independent calls) = **432 assigned API calls**, yielding 288 final arm outputs. Request `gpt-4.1-nano-2025-04-14` as a dated snapshot and `gpt-6-luna` and `gpt-6-sol` as aliases; the latter two are **not immutable snapshot pins**. Record each returned `response.model`, provider request ID and UTC time. Isolate any alias drift as a separate cohort and do not pool identities without an amendment. Run each case once per model and arm, with randomised case and arm order in fresh conversations. `direct` makes one model independently analyse the evidence. `independent_challenge` makes two separate mechanism and rival analyst calls **without either seeing the other's answer**, then a deterministic agreement-only synthesis emits `UNDECIDED` on disagreement. The calls are executed serially in the shuffled schedule; latency includes both. Preserve both analyst records. A service tier or marketing name is **not** a verified small-language-model parameter count. A failed request, refusal, malformed answer, truncation or different returned snapshot remains in the assigned-arm denominator and has a separate failure code. Do not silently retry or replace it.

Before the full schedule, run a **predeclared 72-call pilot** on the first two distinct families in manifest order, including every variant, three requested models and both arms. The gate examines only endpoint availability, schema compliance, source validity, response identity, billing usage, log integrity and whether the frozen arm information is equivalent; it does not tune prompts or select a model by observed accuracy. Cap actual reported API charges at **USD 5 for this pilot** and **USD 25 cumulatively for Stage A**. Freeze a conservative next-call cost reserve before each request; if estimated cost or unreported usage makes the cap unverifiable, stop before a new call and retain the incomplete attempts. These caps are spending ceilings, not estimates of model prices. If preflight projects that the fixed schedule cannot fit the cap, report the infeasible plan rather than silently shrinking the arms or extending the ceiling. Pilot calls may be included in the 432-call developmental schedule only if fixtures, prompts, model identity and scorer remain unchanged; otherwise amend the protocol and start a new cohort. A partial run is labelled feasibility, never a completed 432-call comparison.

**Stage B: topology and representation ablations.** Because Stage A exposes all twelve families, reserve **new, untouched development families** for this stage; no paraphrase or variant of an A family enters B. Cross selected recorded model identities with (a) direct critic, (b) an equally budgeted repeated single-agent/self-consistency route, (c) independent evidence verifier plus bias/rival analyst with deterministic synthesis, and (d) open debate. Give every route the same source and tool permissions; match total model-call and completion-token budgets for an isolated topology contrast, while also reporting each route's natural operating cost. Compare plain EAL/2 with a mechanically derived, meaning-equivalent JSON graph using verified bidirectional content checks. A terse claim/evidence account that omits declarations is a **content ablation**, not a representation-only comparison. A checked host packet is another information factor, as it supplies an answer. Cross factors in a balanced incomplete schedule if a full factorial exceeds the frozen cost cap; declare cells and estimands before running. Do not infer a syntax effect if renderings differ in length, field names or information. Open debate is an experimental comparator, not an assumed improvement.

**Stage C: independent confirmation.** Recruit and freeze at least 48 new argument families, across several authors and engineering domains, with matched variants and independent gold review before finalising prompts or selecting the winner. Split by **family and author**, with no paraphrase, source revision, derived graph, reviewer trace or answer-key exposure across development and confirmation. Predefine one primary contrast (selected topology versus direct, within the selected model) and one safety contrast (false attribution); rank models on the confirmation distribution only if its precision supports the stated decision. Additional models and prompts are exploratory or require a fresh cohort. The number 48 is a proposed recruitment target, not a power claim: simulate detectable differences and interval coverage using family-level intraclass correlation and plausible error rates before declaring readiness.

**Stage D: prospective human study.** Recruit engineers, record a pre-assistance judgement, confidence, evidence searched and grounds, then randomise content-matched conditions by person or task block: EAL/2 alone, ordinary prose alone, EAL/2 plus a structural challenge, prose plus the **same** challenge, and an agent-enabled condition once its safety is established. Independently randomise correct and deliberately flawed AI recommendations; cross fluent versus plain style while preserving factual propositions and evidence identity, with a no-AI baseline where feasible. This factorial structure can distinguish representation, challenge, fluency and agent support rather than crediting all to EAL/2. Assess correct detection and correction, acceptance of wrong advice, unnecessary rejection of correct advice, independent disconfirming search, calibrated confidence, elapsed time, perceived burden and retained performance on later unaided tasks. Ex-ante quality assessors are blind to assistance arm and subsequent outcome. Process assessors inspect the time-ordered interaction, including when outcome information became available, while masked to condition identifiers where the record permits. Analyse assignment effects by participant and task; counterbalance crossover order only if carryover and learning are measured. Obtain applicable ethics review and consent before recruiting. No API-only result can replace this stage or justify the claim that EAL/2 mitigates a human cognitive bias.

## Response contract and grading

Each developmental analyst returns one machine-readable record per case: `status`, `mechanism_id` or `none`, `evidence_ids`, `rival_id`, `defeater_id`, `defect_id`, `correction_id`, `confidence` and a short `rationale`; `case_id` and arm metadata remain in the outer call record. `evidence_ids` is an object with arrays for exposure, opportunity, uptake, signature, temporal order, contrast and rivals. The harness checks valid IDs and its author-labelled role requirements. These structural checks do not validate the truth of the synthetic records or establish a real person's cognitive process. The rationale must distinguish the engineering correction from psychological attribution; blinded reviewers should inspect categorical accusations even when a weak status is returned. The application may enforce the structural rule that status cannot exceed available evidence; this rule and its rejection rate are part of the tested topology, not an unseen oracle.

Before examining outputs, two independent domain reviewers should label and justify the confirmation reference from the **brief and time-ordered evidence**, masked to model, arm, candidate answer and outcome where possible. Report raw agreement and disagreements by status and mechanism; a third reviewer adjudicates with written grounds, preserving both initial labels. If reviewers cannot agree whether process evidence establishes uptake or an admissible rational rival, accept a set of statuses or mark the case `UNDECIDED`; do not resolve ambiguity by majority vote of agents. Audit all cases scored as false accusation or harmful correction and a preselected sample of successes. Automated fixture keys are useful calibration checks, not independent psychological adjudication.

The primary developmental measures are (1) **fully warranted disposition**, requiring permitted status, correct mechanism or explicit non-attribution, traceable required evidence and a correct treatment of the closest rival; (2) **strong false attribution** among `rival`, `missing_link` and `insufficient` cases; and (3) **introduced engineering error**. In Stage A the last measure means selecting an unsafe correction option or adding an unsupported factual assertion; a separate valid-argument case in Stage B/C is required to measure wrongful overturning of a correct decision. `F_strong=1` if a negative/insufficient case receives `EPISODE-SUPPORTED`, `COMPARATIVELY-SUPPORTED` or `CAUSALLY-SUPPORTED` for a mechanism the record does not support, including an asserted wrong mechanism at that strength. Merely naming a `HYPOTHESISED` or `COMPATIBLE` possibility **with explicit uncertainty** is not `F_strong` when the record permits it. For `REJECTED` or `NOT-APPLICABLE` gold, even a weak status that ignores the defeating record is a separate **strict no-accusation violation**. Blind reviewers also code categorical accusatory rationale prose regardless of the nominal weak status; an automated phrase flag selects review items but cannot decide their truth. Report these rates separately. Secondary measures include defect-correction accuracy, status-only accuracy, abstention appropriateness, **supported-case recall, non-`UNDECIDED` coverage and false attribution at matched coverage**, evidence-span precision/recall, confidence calibration, relevant-evidence sensitivity, unsupported additions, request success, input/output/cached tokens, end-to-end money, human reviewer minutes and wall time. Human reviewer time is unknown in an API-only run, not zero. Stage A's between-family evidence-order balance is descriptive and confounded with family; only a separately frozen within-family order swap can estimate order susceptibility.

## Estimands, analysis and decisions

Let $i$ index independent case **families**, $v$ a matched variant, $m$ a recorded model identity and $t$ a topology. Let $Y_{iv}(m,t)$ indicate fully warranted disposition, $F_{iv}(m,t)$ strong false attribution where eligible, and $H_{iv}(m,t)$ an induced engineering error. The primary paired contrast for selected topology $t_1$ versus direct $t_0$, on the declared family distribution, is

\[
\Delta_Y=E_i\!\left[\overline Y_i(m,t_1)-\overline Y_i(m,t_0)\right],\qquad
\Delta_F=E_i\!\left[\overline F_i(m,t_1)-\overline F_i(m,t_0)\right].
\]

Variant averages are taken **within** family; repeated calls refine response-stability estimates and never increase the family count. For cue $q$, Stage B can measure a paired verdict or confidence shift $D_i(m,t,q)$ between cue-present and cue-absent records when all relevant evidence is fixed. Record a separate response to the relevant-evidence pair. An irrelevant-cue effect is an experimentally identified model-system response under consistency and successful stimulus matching; it does not reveal a human's mechanism. The Stage A variants and between-family order counterbalance **do not identify such a cue effect**. Mechanism attribution in an archival human case is partially identified or undecided when uptake, opportunity or credible rivals are missing.

Use case-family paired effects, family-level randomisation/permutation intervals or a hierarchical binomial model with family and author effects when information supports it. Present uncertainty for each model and topology, and a dependence-aware interval for their differences. Do not calculate ordinary call-level binomial intervals as if 48 variants or repeated model calls were independent cases. Forty-eight independent families cannot in general exclude a five-percentage-point safety excess: even **zero** errors in 48 independent eligible families has a two-sided 95% exact upper bound above 5%; roughly 72 zero-error independent opportunities would be needed merely for that absolute-risk bound, and paired differences with nonzero errors can require more. Predeclare the analysis code and transformations before the confirmation cohort; retain all attempted assignments and analyse system success including failures, with an additional conditional-on-response diagnostic. Control multiplicity for the nominated primary family of contrasts (for example, Holm adjustment) and mark all further model, mechanism and prompt searches exploratory. Report the detected effect range from a simulation incorporating family clustering; an inconclusive interval is an inconclusive result, not evidence of equivalence.

A topology is a **candidate for human testing** only if its independent-family interval supports a practically meaningful gain in $Y$, its upper uncertainty bound excludes a predeclared unacceptable increase in $F$ and $H$, its supported-case recall and coverage meet their predeclared minimum, and its cost and latency fit the declared application limits. Before confirmation, elicit those limits from the intended engineering setting; the illustrative five-percentage-point excess false-attribution margin is not justified as a universal safety threshold. If checks fail, retain the simpler direct or human-only process while examining the failure. A model is called the best **of tested models for the stated loss function, task distribution and date** only when its held-out performance and total cost support that decision; otherwise report a Pareto set and uncertainty. The OpenAI key alone supplies no evidence about open-weights SLMs, whose exact weights, quantisation, hardware and runtime would require a separately frozen comparison.

The decision matrix is: supported directional contrast with valid reference and no safety excess → advance only the bounded agent-design claim; no contrast or wide interval → inconclusive; false attribution or introduced-error excess → reject that route for this setting; relevant-evidence insensitivity → reject cue-invariance interpretation; reference disagreement or leaked gold → suspend affected families; model identity drift or changed source → stop and start a new frozen cohort. An API-only success cannot advance the human-mitigation claim. A synthetic bias label cannot advance a claim about real reviewers.

## Evidence informing the design

Buçinca, Malaya and Gajos, *To Trust or to Think*, *Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction* 5(CSCW1), 2021, DOI [10.1145/3449287](https://doi.org/10.1145/3449287), found that cognitive forcing reduced overreliance in their human task while lowering subjective ratings for the most forcing designs; transfer to engineering review remains to be tested. Vasconcelos et al., *Explanations Can Reduce Overreliance on AI Systems During Decision-Making*, [arXiv:2212.06823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06823), experimentally varied verification costs and benefits, offering a serious alternative to treating every unverified recommendation as a cognitive disposition. Bansal et al., *Does the Whole Exceed its Parts?*, CHI 2021, DOI [10.1145/3411764.3445717](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445717), found that explanations increased acceptance of both correct and incorrect advice without improving complementary team performance in their tested tasks. Steyvers et al., *What large language models know and what people think they know*, *Nature Machine Intelligence* 2025, DOI [10.1038/s42256-024-00976-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00976-7), found that longer explanations raised human confidence without improving their discrimination between correct and incorrect answers. Cheikes et al., *An Empirical Evaluation of Structured Argumentation Using the Toulmin Argument Formalism*, [MITRE 2004](https://www.mitre.org/news-insights/publication/empirical-evaluation-structured-argumentation-using-toulmin-argument), found mixed effects in two article-evaluation tasks and difficulty/variation in authored structures. Smit et al., *Should we be going MAD?*, ICML 2024, [PMLR 235](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/smit24a.html), did not find reliable multi-agent debate superiority over strong self-consistency and ensembling comparators. Ma et al., *Judging with Many Minds*, Findings of EMNLP 2025, DOI [10.18653/v1/2025.findings-emnlp.941](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-emnlp.941), observed amplification of position, verbosity, chain-of-thought and bandwagon effects in their debate setting. Shi et al., *Judging the Judges*, IJCNLP-AACL 2025, DOI [10.18653/v1/2025.ijcnlp-long.18](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.ijcnlp-long.18), found model- and task-dependent position sensitivity. Sharma et al., *Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models*, [arXiv:2310.13548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548), motivate an explicit user-preference perturbation. These studies motivate arms and negative controls; none establishes an EAL/2 benefit.
