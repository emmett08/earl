# Pre-call amendment 0.1.1 — bias-agent developmental study

**Date:** 24 September 2026 UTC. **Status:** fixed before any paid call. This
amends `PROTOCOL.md` version 0.1.0 without changing the 12 families, four
classes, three requested models, two Stage A routes, 72-call pilot, 432-call
full schedule or their USD 5 and USD 25 ceilings. The original zero-call
freezes remain byte-exact historical records; they must not be executed with
this source. The amended freezes have separate names in the result archive.

The earlier generated request exposed `__process`, `__rival`,
`__missing_link` or `__insufficient` through its case ID and EAL scope. Each
request now uses a stable opaque episode identifier derived from the private
case ID. The internal schedule and ledger still retain the family and variant
for paired analysis. Every EAL source now declares one common candidate
objection against the process hypothesis, bound to a neutral `E0` record.
The objection's presence no longer identifies a negative case, and its
authored presence is not a host verdict. The absent-exposure variant no longer
introduces an additional no-opportunity record. Defect, correction and rival
option identifiers and display order now vary by case; the gold mapping stays
private. Distractors are closer to plausible engineering actions, although
this authored calibration still needs independent content review and harder
held-out cases before any generalisation.

The primary `fully_warranted` score and paired topology contrast use the raw
direct answer or the raw deterministic synthesis of two independent answers.
The author-labelled `role_evidence` gate is now reported separately as
`gated_fully_warranted`, `gated_positive_supported_recall`,
`gated_decisive_coverage` and `negative_published_false_attribution`. It has
access to private fixture roles and can force strong attribution to zero on
constructed negatives by design. It is an **oracle-assisted diagnostic**, not
a deployable safety control or evidence that the model recognised human bias.
A deployable gate would have to derive and validate roles from the public
records, then undergo independent reference and error testing.

Independent synthesis now intersects each analyst's cited IDs by role. It
cannot assemble a complete process chain by combining two individually
incomplete chains. The analyser retains analyst-level strong false attribution
and wrong correction counts before synthesis, as well as raw route outputs,
so consensus and the private gate cannot conceal an analyst's harmful answer.
Categorical accusations in rationale prose still require blinded manual
review; the script does not decide their truth. Stage A remains confounded by
one versus two model calls. A call-matched repetition in Stage B is needed to
attribute any difference to topology.

The full runner now requires exactly the complete first-two-family pilot in
its prior ledger. It locks both pilot and full ledgers while deciding which
calls to omit, so a concurrently running pilot cannot cause duplicate paid
assignments. Provider failures and unresolved requests continue to stop
without retry. Offline tests cover leakage, scaffold uniformity, shuffled
answers, raw versus gated scoring, shared citations, analyst harm, schedule
integrity and the two-ledger gate. No human or model outcome is introduced by
this amendment.
