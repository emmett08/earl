The three designs answer different questions. A gain from EAL host against raw prompting identifies a **system allocation effect** conditional on a faithful source and trusted observations. A matched EAL/equal-checker contrast tests representation, source authoring, revision, trace or integration costs after both hosts receive equal semantics. Correct endpoint parity is the expected result when two equally correct implementations execute the same graph; it is no evidence for unique EAL reasoning power. A recipient may repeat a host-supplied status without understanding or faithfully explaining it.

The newer `benchmarks/experiments/cross-model-developmental.json` and `cross-model-cohort-developmental.json` freeze 180 and 135 initial recipient cases, respectively, with up to 36 and 27 extra routed turns. Their four-root historical and three-root new synthetic corpora are developmental. The new corpus has an independent brief-level *code* oracle but no second masked human adjudication; the four-root archive was exposed in an earlier pilot. `skill_route` is a model-originated text JSON request to a validating host, not a native MCP function call. It selects a known artefact ID rather than using `FamilyRegistry` or `CandidateIndex`. The schedules do not instantiate any of the older 96-, 800- or 960-call designs. The stopped pending attempt in `benchmarks/results/2026-09-24-cross-model-offline/status.json` has unknown billing and must remain separate from a fresh paid run.

## Claim and exposure contracts for new work

Keep EAL/2 source syntax and reasoning grammar unchanged. Freeze four distinct interventions rather than calling them all “use EAL”:

1. **Raw or instructed interpretation:** a recipient sees the same formal source and records; an instruction-only arm adds a short, matched application procedure. Its answer is the recipient's answer.
2. **Model-requested assessment:** a recipient requests an exact authorised claim; a host validates the request, assesses a pinned artefact and supplies a compact packet on a second turn. Report request rate, wrong selection, no request, retries, second-turn tokens and recipient explanation. A text JSON request is not evidence of native MCP competence.
3. **Host-initiated assessment:** an authenticated application resolves an approved task, assesses one selected claim and owns the final status. The recipient can explain the packet but cannot replace its status. Compare equal EAL and generic graph hosts with the same authorisation, acquisition, answer gate, method semantics, packet detail and cache policy.
4. **Candidate family selection:** `CandidateIndex` proposes ranked *family IDs* from task text. An authorised, reviewed exact typed tuple must then resolve to a pinned artefact and selected claim through `FamilyRegistry`; a candidate score never supplies bindings, a verdict or permission. Compare exact trusted task metadata against candidate-assisted choice and record ambiguous ties, misses, wrong families, wrong tuples and denial. Candidate retrieval is an upstream workflow exposure, not a source of evidence for a claim.

For each root, pair at least three changes to admissible evidence under the **same** claim, context, scope and interval, and separately test a changed claim or scope. The latter requires an additional reviewed artefact or refusal; it must not inherit a status by changing a string parameter. A family catalogue is a finite index over reviewed tuples, not a proof schema for arbitrary Kubernetes namespaces or resource limits. Its review checksum pins a contract but cannot establish the reviewer identity or the truth of an authored engineering warrant. The present `TaskFamilyHost` issuance record is process-local; a remote or restartable study must bind durable assessment identity to authenticated principal, task, artefact, claim, source, observations and time, or reassess after restart. Supplied evidence flags remain assertions until acquisition provenance is independently checked.

## Revised estimands and scoring

For a fixed selected-root set, report paired root-weighted differences rather than treating repeated states and recipients as independent tasks. Declare before calls the following distinct quantities:

* **Accepted-status error:** difference in exact reference-matched host-owned decisions between raw recipient, instructed recipient, model-requested host, EAL host and equal host, with an explicit unavailable result when routing, identity or collection fails. A raw answer is assessed as the raw route's decision; the host route is assessed by its actual host final status, regardless of recipient prose.
* **False support and revision:** probability of an accepted `supported` label lacking admissible support under each state, plus correct support-to-contested/unsupported-to-restored sequences by root. Malformed and provider failures remain attempted failures; separately bound their possible false-support contribution rather than calling them safe abstentions. Report false abstention too.
* **Explanation fidelity:** masked reviewers check selected claim and scope, admitted evidence identity, method result and qualification, decisive route, applicable objection/defence, and unsupported additions. Score recipient prose separately from host status. A packet that states the answer is a delivery treatment, not proof of recipient reasoning.
* **Retrieval and binding:** candidate recall at a declared `k`, correct family/tuple/claim choice, ambiguity and refusal, authorisation violations, and elapsed time/token cost from task receipt to accepted status. Include similar lexical decoys, unseen tuples, new-scope tasks and stale catalogue revisions. Never allow retrieval output to write grants.
* **All-in cost per correctly accepted decision:** numerator is actual provider charges across every attempted author, review, revision and recipient call, human source/catalogue review time at declared rates, evidence acquisition, host/checker compute and transport, and deployed explanation review where required. Denominator counts accepted decisions with the correct status, authorised binding and required faithful explanation. State the recipient count and source/scope turnover; give costs and successful counts separately, with uncertainty and `unavailable` if any required labour or charge is unknown. Research-only outcome adjudication is separately itemised unless it is part of the deployed workflow. Provider tokens per correct, input/output/cached/reasoning usage, retries, wall latency and sequential tool round trips remain separate measures.

Do not divide mean route cost by mean route correctness and interpret it as a per-task causal effect. Aggregate costs and successes under the same frozen allocation and report root-level paired uncertainty; a zero-success route has undefined cost per correct. Keep the old 800-call provider-token ratio as a secondary historical estimand if its exact protocol is later run. The new all-in monetary estimand requires actual time/rate measurements and cannot be calculated from packet bytes or token estimates alone. If many recipients use one source, report the observed `N` and a sensitivity curve for assumed future `N`, charging every new scope or source revision its own review cost.

## An executable next cohort

A *separate developmental* family-routing pilot can start from at least eight genealogically distinct ordinary briefs, each with three same-scope evidence states and one changed-scope query backed by another reviewed artefact or an expected refusal. Include at least two plausible candidate families for ambiguous tasks. Freeze EAL and independently authored generic graphs from each brief, an independent reference before model outputs, exact query texts, all acquisition envelopes, review records, complete prompts, model identities, schedules and scorer. Cross three eligible recipient model snapshots with six delivery arms: raw, instruction only, exact-ID model request, candidate-assisted model request, preassessed EAL host and preassessed equal host. For `8 × 4 × 3 × 6`, this is **576 first-turn calls**, plus at most **192** second turns if both request arms complete. It is a proposed maximum of 768 model calls before authoring calls, not a relabelled execution of the old 800-call pilot or a power justification. First-turn failures remain in assigned denominators. If the strong reasoning model lacks a native tool interface, use the same external text-request route and reserve a separate native MCP experiment for tool-capable products with a genuinely matched interface.

Before any paid confirmatory comparison, two independent masked reviewers must adjudicate every brief, EAL source, generic graph, status and consequential warrant; disputes stay unresolved until adjudicated, not silently assigned an interpreter label. Freeze a comparator in the common supported semantic fragment and run both implementations on valid, stale, future, wrong-scope, wrong-tool, missing-record, forged/replayed and restored variants. Review the independent checker source and record its hash. Freeze family catalogues and exact typed cases after review. Verify source provenance and tool acquisition separately from the graph evaluator. Then record a provider/model capability probe, current prices, reserved maximum cost, rate limits, no-silent-retry rule, immutable prompt/order hashes and secret scan. A failed oracle, parity, authorisation, billing or acquisition gate suspends the affected contrast. A changed fixture, model, grammar, scorer or primary estimand starts a new version and result directory.

The current offline tests and synthetic parity checks can be replayed now. The 180- and 135-case developmental campaigns may be run as distinct paid exploratory studies after a fresh execution freeze and operational approval; they cannot supply missing human review or turn into the older protocols after responses arrive. Model outcomes, faithful explanation, total campaign tokens, latency and all-in cost remain unmeasured at this audit cut. A confirmatory EAL-specific or Kubernetes reliability claim needs independently reviewed new roots, validated live acquisition and a separately frozen analysis with information sized to the practical effect.
