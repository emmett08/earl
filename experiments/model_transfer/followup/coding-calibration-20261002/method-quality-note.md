# Coder calibration method and quality note

This exercise prospectively freezes 24 newly authored English fictional answers for the existing whole-answer rubric. It supports ASM-003, CTRL-005 and launch review LA-S07. It does not establish that either actual coder has passed: no coder output was available when these files were written.

The public bundle has the annotation export schema, the exact combined decision and consistency rubric, opaque nonsequential IDs, and blank fields for both labels, quotes and notes. It contains no task IDs, condition labels, reference decisions, coverage categories or gold rationale. Its shuffled order is frozen. The separately sealed key supplies expected semantic labels and rationale; private fictional task facts appear only for three adverse wrong-fact examples. Those facts demonstrate why an internally coherent answer can be factually wrong. They are never given to a coder or used as a factual-scoring axis.

Coverage includes explicit current ready/not_ready/undetermined conclusions in prose and JSON; incompatible current canonical/prose and prose/prose verdicts; superseded history; hypothetical future outcomes with clear current conclusions; withholding approval because readiness is unknown; a known mandatory failure alongside another unknown; deliberately tentative modal ambiguity; no task decision; a bare decision without explanation; and incorrect but internally coherent factual assertions for each of the three verdicts. The two deliberate modal examples test preservation of unresolved wording, not selection of a favorable reading. Other examples use direct current or temporal qualifications to reduce avoidable gold disputes.

Gold labels concern communicated meaning only. A current JSON ready field retained alongside a current failed-criterion prose conclusion is decision-ambiguous and explanation-contradictory. A past or hypothetical opposite verdict is not a contradiction when expressly distinguished from the present one. “Not ready (undetermined)” is unknown-based withholding when its full explanation makes that meaning explicit. A request to resend the question communicates no task decision, while a bare decision communicates a verdict without explanation. Factual truth is not inferred from measurements.

Before participant coding, give only the public bundle to two fresh AI coders with separate contexts. Preserve their original outputs and evaluate both only after completion. Each must attain exact 24/24 decision and 24/24 consistency agreement and valid relevant exact quotes in all 48 quote fields. Complete IDs, unchanged texts and nonempty notes are also required. The prospective protocol states all acceptance and retention requirements; correctly coded ambiguous examples count as successes, while disagreement about the gold remains a failure of this exercise.

Any error or missing exercise records a fail and suspends semantic participant coding and substantive-feasibility interpretation; canonical counts may be retained separately. Repair requires a prospectively declared change and a new independently authored held-out set, never retrospective relabeling or reuse after answer disclosure. If a gold defect is alleged, retain the failed attempt and suspend rather than changing this key to manufacture a pass.

The author inspected AGENTS.md, the source annotation rubrics/export schema, the governing protocol records and the launch scientific review. No live participant artifacts, rows, masked items or outcomes were inspected. A separate AI design consultant reviewed only the authorized source documents and returned rubric edge-case guidance; it did not inspect the authored fixtures or sealed key. No claim of human validation, independent investigator replication, factual grounding or general coder reliability is made. A pass would be bounded evidence of applying this rubric to this authored exercise, with shared AI error and novel-wording uncertainty still possible.
